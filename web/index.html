THE RULES

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Choose an era. Legends combines earlier careers; All eras mixes the full pool.
  2. Draw your touchline. One manager and a compatible formation come as a pair.
  3. Spin five times. Each spin awards three distinct players. All fifteen are yours.
  4. Pick your eleven. The engine assigns roles, leaving four substitutes. Swap compatible bench players in.
  5. Play. Run one match, repeat 2,000 times, or try a 38-match gauntlet against generated squads.
  6. Experiment in the notebook. Inspect the data, edit weights, import EA or PES exports, and analyze real events.

These game ratings and tactical rules do not establish who would win a real historical match. The project is independent of Eraball, EA, FIFA, and Konami.
