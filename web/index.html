THE RULES

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Choose an era. All eras mixes the pool. Randomize Era chooses one eligible era before drafting.
  2. Draw your touchline. One manager and a compatible formation come as a pair.
  3. Spin five times. Each spin awards three distinct people. All fifteen are yours.
  4. Pick your eleven. The engine assigns roles and leaves four substitutes. Only compatible swaps are accepted.
  5. Take the pitch. Classic matches are experiments. Challenge, gauntlet, and league conditions are recorded with your run.
  6. Play another human. Head to Head supports two players on this device or a server room. Both players must finish drafting and confirm readiness.

Notebook and browser use the same game engine. These ratings and tactical assumptions do not establish who would win a real historical match. Rankings are limited to this server. This project is independent of Eraball, EA, FIFA, and Konami.
