# Ratings provenance

`players.json` is a curated subset of community-published ratings. Its source records, declared licences, retrieval date, and SHA-256 are in `manifest.json`. The full downloaded archives are not part of this repository.

The dataset publishers declare CC0: Public Domain. Their declarations apply to the supplied datasets; they do not imply permission to reuse game artwork, logos, portraits, or trademarks. The project copies numeric ratings and player facts, with source attribution. The project's MIT licence applies to its own code.

Historical base ICON/HERO cards retain the FC 24 numeric attributes. Broad career-era tags are curated metadata and must not be interpreted as dated measurements. FIFA 18 and FC 24 snapshot ratings are edition-specific. Detailed attributes missing from the provider remain `null`.

Put your own EA or PES exports and downloaded event files in `local/`. This directory is ignored. No credentials or account tokens are needed by the game or ratings loaders.
