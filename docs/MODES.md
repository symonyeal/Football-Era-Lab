# Keep playing with the drafted squad

The season results screen offers Era Gauntlet and a tournament circuit. Both keep the same fifteen people, formation and manager. The simulation decade changes, so the same cards receive a new era adjustment. Every match calls the same engine as the league and Cup. These modes do not add fictional training improvements or replace your players.

## Era Gauntlet

The browser starts the Gauntlet in the 1950s. The boss is the highest-rated available opponent squad in that decade. Play one neutral knockout match, with extra time and penalties if needed. Win to move to the next decade; lose to keep the same squad and boss for another attempt with a new deterministic match seed. The Gauntlet ends after you beat the boss in all eight decades.

The strongest boss comes from the supplied ranked pool and engine rating, not an independently established all-time best club. Retry counts and the match log record the attempts. Bench availability and fatigue are simulated during a match; the lineup remains the same between attempts.

## Tournament circuit

Choose 10 to 20 events. The circuit starts in your season's simulation decade, advances one decade per event and wraps through the eight supported decades. Five formats repeat in this order:

| Format | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| Eight-club league | Your club plus seven opponents play home and away. The final table decides the title. |
| European Cup | Sixteen clubs play two-legged ties through the semi-finals and a neutral final. |
| Eight-club knockout | Quarter-finals, semi-finals and a neutral final; tied matches use extra time and penalties. |
| Champions groups | Sixteen clubs enter four groups of four. Groups play home and away; the top two in each enter an eight-club knockout. |
| Era Super Cup | Your club plays the strongest selected opponent in one neutral knockout final. |

Opponents are chosen by their engine strength within the decade's supplied field. A title comes from that event's final table or knockout champion, and circuit totals come from the simulated fixture results. Goals, assists, appearances and clean sheets come from the same match events. The circuit does not award a title from an expected win probability or an overall-rating comparison alone.

The pool, estimated historical ratings and game coefficients carry the same limits as the [season model](MODEL.md). Head to Head, Weekly Challenge, the fictional career and mini-games are outside these active v2 rules.
