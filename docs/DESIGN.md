# How the game plays

The browser offers eight simulation decades, 1950s through 2020s, plus a random choice. This sets the opponents and scoring environment; draft spins can draw cards from any supported decade.

1. Spin five distinct managers with a formation associated with each manager. Choose one combination. There are two manager re-spins before you choose.
2. Spin a club and decade. The browser lists the full available squad, with positions, base rating, source, apportioned appearances and goals, and tags. Select three undrafted people, placing each in an empty pitch or bench slot. The preview shows his position and era adjustment. One squad re-spin is available for the whole draft, before making a pick from that spin.
3. Repeat for five squad spins, giving fifteen distinct people. A person already drafted remains unavailable in every other club and decade. Swap slots before kick-off. The eleven pitch slots follow your chosen formation, and the four bench slots carry no position penalty.
4. Play a league with nineteen clubs from the simulation decade. Everyone plays everyone home and away, so your club plays 38 matches. The other clubs have their own cards, managers and formations and play one another.
5. Play the European Cup with fifteen other clubs. Round of 16, quarter-finals and semi-finals have two legs; the final is one match at a neutral ground. A tied aggregate or final is decided by extra time and penalties.

The seed determines draws and match randomness with unchanged data, engine and settings. A seed by itself does not identify your choices or replay results after the model or data changes. Saved browser progress belongs to the browser using it.

Club-decade squads combine qualifying stints across the whole decade. Teammates who played at different times can appear together. Opponent ranking uses available domestic results and European Cup records; it is a declared construction of the game, not an official Europe-wide league table. The simulated league and Cup use one modern competition format in every decade.

The game borrows Eraball's era choice, spin-and-select rhythm, free slot placement, position-fit penalty, cross-era adjustment, manager signature-player upgrade and knockout experience idea. Football positions and match outcomes use this project's explicit formulas. This is a football adaptation with its own interface and assets; exact parity with every Eraball feature is not claimed.

After a season, Era Gauntlet and the tournament circuit use the completed squad and the same match engine. The Gauntlet lets you retry a decade's strongest club after a loss; the circuit rotates five formats across decades for 10 to 20 events. Their [rules are documented separately](MODES.md). Head to Head and Weekly Challenge remain future work. V1's career, mini-games and rooms are available as archived implementation records, not active v2 choices.
